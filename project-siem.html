Custom SIEM & Security Operations Dashboard

Real-time threat detection and automated response, built from scratch and running local-first with no external data leakage.

01 / OVERVIEW

The problem

Off-the-shelf detection stacks are heavy, and they tend to ship logs off to someone else's cloud. I wanted a lean, transparent system I fully controlled: something that could watch a host in real time, make deterministic decisions, and act on threats without waiting on a human or a third party.

What it does

  • Ingests and analyzes logs in real time against a configurable rule engine.
  • Blocks malicious traffic automatically through direct iptables integration.
  • Surfaces everything on a live terminal dashboard so state is visible at a glance.
  • Raises configurable alerts so the important events do not get lost in volume.

The approach

The goal was to shorten the time between a threat appearing and a response landing. Detection logic is deterministic and rule-driven, so its decisions are auditable rather than a black box. Everything runs locally by design, keeping sensitive log data on the machine rather than shipping it anywhere.
