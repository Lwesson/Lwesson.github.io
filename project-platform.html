AI-Augmented Security Testing Platform

A phase-driven testing platform that brings LLM reasoning into each stage of an authorized engagement while enforcing scope at every step.

OVERVIEW

The problem

Security testing produces a flood of raw output: scan results, endpoints, responses, and half-signals that a human has to reason over under time pressure. The reasoning is the slow part, and it is where scope discipline usually slips. I wanted a system that could accelerate the reasoning without ever letting testing drift outside the rules of engagement.

What it does

The platform runs an engagement as a sequence of discrete phases. Each phase has a defined input, a defined output, and a guardrail check before anything proceeds. Large language model APIs sit inside the pipeline to do the analytical heavy lifting:

  • Attack-surface reasoning over reconnaissance data to prioritize where to look next.
  • Finding triage that separates real signal from noise and ranks by likely impact.
  • Vulnerability-chain analysis that connects individual findings into plausible end-to-end paths.

Underneath, it orchestrates standard Linux security tooling as controlled subprocesses, capturing evidence to disk as it goes.

The approach

The design principle is that honesty has to be structural, not aspirational. Scope-aware guardrails enforce the rules of engagement at every phase, so the system cannot act outside what was authorized. Evidence is written to disk before any finding is drafted, so conclusions are always traceable back to raw data. Findings carry explicit labels distinguishing what is directly exploitable from what only primes a follow-on step.
